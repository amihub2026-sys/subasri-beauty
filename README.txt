SUBASRI BEAUTY MADURAI — STATIC WEBSITE

QUICK START
Extract the ZIP, open the subasri-beauty folder, and double-click index.html.
No build tools, installation, external fonts or internet connection are needed.
Upload index.html, style.css, script.js and the assets folder together to any static host.

FILES
index.html — all single-page content and contact form
style.css — pink/gold styling, desktop and mobile layout, animation
script.js — logo entrance, mobile navigation, scroll reveals, gallery viewer, enquiry preparation
assets/ — included logo and illustrative beauty images in WebP format

CONTACT / BOOKINGS
No phone number, full address or booking endpoint was supplied. The form prepares an enquiry that the visitor can copy; it does not send a message or confirm an appointment. Before public launch, replace this workflow with your actual WhatsApp contact or form service and add your real contact details in the contact section.

IMAGES
Beauty photographs are AI-generated concept images, extracted from the approved design direction, and are labelled as illustrative inspiration. Replace with your studio's actual portfolio photographs before representing them as your own work. Original uploaded logo is included. Replace images by keeping the same asset filenames or updating the matching paths in index.html.

ANIMATION / ACCESSIBILITY
Logo opens in the centre then moves to the navbar. Visitors requesting reduced motion skip the intro and decorative animation. The site includes keyboard navigation, form labels, focus styles, alternative text and an Escape-close gallery dialog.

CUSTOMISE
Change colours in the :root block at the beginning of style.css.
Edit service descriptions and business details in index.html.
